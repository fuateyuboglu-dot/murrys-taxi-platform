# Product Principles

## One Tap Philosophy

Every common action should be achievable in one tap whenever possible.

---

## Customer First

The customer should never wonder what happens next.

Every screen answers:

- Where is my driver?
- How long?
- What is happening?

---

## Driver First

Drivers earn money, not learn software.

Everything must work with minimal interaction.

---

## Dispatcher First

Dispatchers should never need more than a few seconds to assign a ride.

---

## Local Taxi First

We are NOT Uber.

We empower local taxi companies.

---

## Simplicity Wins

If a feature requires explanation,
it is too complicated.

---

## Premium Feeling

Fast animations.

Large touch targets.

Clear typography.

Minimal screens.

Apple-level polish.

---

## Offline Friendly

The app should gracefully handle weak internet whenever possible.

---

## Accessibility

Large fonts.

High contrast.

Simple language.

Easy for elderly users.

---

## Scalable

Every feature should work for:

- 1 taxi
- 10 taxis
- 100 taxis
- 1000 taxis

without redesign.

---

## Build Once

Every feature should be reusable.

No duplicated logic.

No duplicated UI.

No duplicated business rules.
