## Version
v1.0

---

# Product

Murrys Taxi

A modern taxi dispatch platform for local taxi companies.

The first customer is Murrys Taxi in Arnprior, Ontario.

The platform is designed to support multiple taxi companies in the future.

---

# User Roles

## Customer

Can:

- Register with phone number
- Book a taxi
- Track driver
- View trip history
- Save favorite places
- Contact support

---

## Driver

Can:

- Login
- Go Online / Offline
- Accept jobs
- Navigate to pickup
- Start trip
- End trip
- View completed trips

---

## Dispatcher

Can:

- View incoming requests
- Assign jobs
- Reassign jobs
- Track drivers
- Track active trips
- Contact drivers

A dispatcher can also be a driver.

---

## Administrator

Can:

- Manage company
- Manage drivers
- Manage dispatchers
- Manage vehicles
- View reports
- Configure pricing
